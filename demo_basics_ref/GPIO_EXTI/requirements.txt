[实验名称] GPIO外部中断实验
[功能目标] 按下用户按键（PC13）时触发外部中断，并切换板载 LED（PA5）状态。
[开发板和MCU型号] NUCLEO-G431RB开发板，搭载 STM32G431RBT6 MCU

[Experiment Name] GPIO External Interrupt Experiment
[Objective] Trigger an external interrupt when the user button (PC13) is pressed, and toggle the state of the onboard LED (PA5).
[Board and MCU] NUCLEO-G431RB development board, equipped with the STM32G431RBT6 MCU.
